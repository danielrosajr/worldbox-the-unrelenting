## Overview

**The Unrelenting** introduces a persistent supernatural curse into **WorldBox**. A cursed creature is hunted by an entity that inhabits ordinary creatures already living in the world. It could be a villager, a sheep, a wolf, or another seemingly harmless being.

The entity moves toward its victim, changes hosts under specific circumstances, and kills on contact. Killing the current host or identifying its disguise does not necessarily end the threat. The curse can also spread through violence, traumatic events, and family bloodlines.

Inspired by the unsettling, unavoidable pursuit found in supernatural horror, **The Unrelenting** aims to create emergent horror stories inside a living simulation rather than introduce a conventional monster.

> **The idea:** spotting the suspicious sheep should not make you feel safe. It should make you wonder which creature will follow you next.

## Features

### An ordinary creature, an extraordinary threat

- **Hidden pursuer:** the entity inhabits an existing creature instead of spawning an obvious boss monster.
- **Single active presence:** a single pursuer works through the current queue of cursed victims.
- **Unpredictable movement:** each host can be slow, move at its natural speed, or receive a fast movement trait.
- **Sudden bursts:** occasional speed bursts can turn a seemingly manageable chase into a dangerous one.
- **Anti-kite behavior:** deliberately running in circles near the entity can trigger a rare, exceptionally fast **Punishing Rush**.
- **Adaptive tracking:** the closer the entity gets, the more frequently it updates its target's position.
- **Reduced distractions:** the mod periodically interrupts ordinary host behavior to prioritize the pursuit. Some vanilla AI actions may still occur.

### Body-hopping and isolation

Starting with **v0.14.1 — The Patient Hunter**, the entity no longer changes bodies automatically after a fixed number of seconds.

Instead, a host change may occur when the pursuer **encounters another eligible creature nearby**, after a minimum time in its current body and a probability check. A familiar-looking creature can therefore stop following you while a different one takes its place.

**Geography matters.** In **Manual, Realistic, and Normal** modes, the entity does not automatically possess a distant creature to bypass an ocean or an isolated island. In **Nightmare**, a rare remote rescue can occur after the current host becomes genuinely stuck. Normal WorldBox pathfinding limitations still apply.

### Three recognizable curse traits

| Trait | How it is acquired |
| --- | --- |
| **Haunted** | Applied manually, generated by random Origins, or transferred to a likely killer. |
| **Haunted: Trauma** | Acquired by an eligible nearby witness after a probably violent death. |
| **Haunted: Bloodline** | Inherited by a verified living descendant after an entity-caused execution. |

The traits have **distinct custom icons**, but all three lead to the **same entity, pursuit system, and lethal contact**. The extra labels identify *how* the curse began, not different monster types.

### How the curse spreads

**Origins** — Apply **Haunted** manually, or enable rare automatic origins. Automatic occurrences are configurable, can repeat after a chain ends, and are **off by default** in the base configuration.

**The Witness** — When a Haunted victim dies to another creature, the mod can try to transfer the curse to the **probable killer**. Identification relies on nearby kill-count changes and is intentionally conservative; it is not guaranteed to identify every killer correctly.

**Trauma Witness** — Witnessing a probably violent death may curse **one eligible nearby bystander**, even if the deceased was not Haunted. Probability, radius, and cooldown are configurable. Proximity is used as a proxy for witnessing an event; actual line of sight is not established.

**Bloodline** — If the **entity itself** kills a cursed character, the mod can select **one verified living descendant** to inherit **Haunted: Bloodline**. Children are considered before grandchildren and more distant descendants. If the relationship cannot be established, the mod does not guess.

Transmission systems have safeguards to avoid turning one death into an uncontrolled wave of infections. They can be enabled or disabled individually.

### Immersion and Worldfall integration

**Worldfall is optional.** The core curse works in regular **2D WorldBox**, while Worldfall can provide a more personal experience when directly controlling a character.

Optional immersion features include:

- **Quiet proximity cues:** subtle sounds, whispers, and rare growls that may play when the entity stays dangerously close.
- **Music ducking:** gradual reduction of detected game music during tense moments, with restoration afterward.
- **Visual warning:** a subtle, low-intensity red pulse near the edges of the screen. No rapid flashing is intended.
- **Death jumpscare:** an optional full-screen image and sound, intended **only** when the entity kills the character directly controlled by the player.
- **Audio and visual previews:** test effects without waiting for an actual death.
- **Optional status and diagnostics:** inspect the active preset, curse activity, and development counters without automatically revealing the pursuer's identity.

> **Experimental integration:** Worldfall possession detection, 3D death-screen layering, and music control depend on Worldfall's internal implementation. Visual previews and audio previews may work even when the death overlay does not appear above Worldfall's own death screen. Ordinary deaths should remain unaffected by the entity-specific jumpscare.

## Choose your experience

The mod includes **five Quick Start presets**. Select one from **NeoModLoader → The Unrelenting → QuickStart**, or enable the optional in-game quick panel.

| Preset | Experience | Automatic Origins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 — Manual** | Full control over when the curse begins. Best for experiments and storytelling. | Off |
| **1 — Realistic** | Very rare outbreaks and subtler pursuit. | 0.5% every 600 real seconds |
| **2 — Normal** | A balance of mystery, spread, and danger. | 1.5% every 180 real seconds |
| **3 — Nightmare** | More frequent curses, higher pressure, and rare remote host rescue. | 5% every 90 real seconds |
| **4 — Custom** | Use the detailed settings you have saved. | Configurable |

**Manual is the initial preset.** The other modes apply gameplay values without overwriting the saved **Custom** settings. Percentages are **per eligible attempt**, not guaranteed occurrence rates. A new origin also requires an eligible creature and no conflicting active chain.

Every major system can still be tuned in the advanced configuration groups: `Origins`, `TheWitness`, `TraumaWitness`, `Bloodline`, `PursuitAI`, `AntiKite`, and `Immersion`.

## Requirements

- **WorldBox 0.51.2 (build 719)** — version used during development and testing; other builds are not guaranteed.
- **[NeoModLoader](https://github.com/WorldBoxOpenMods/ModLoader)** — required.
- **Worldfall** — optional, for direct-character-control immersion features.
- Windows or Linux PC. Most practical testing so far has been on **Linux / Steam**.

## Installation

1. Install [NeoModLoader](https://github.com/WorldBoxOpenMods/ModLoader) and enable **Experimental Mode** in WorldBox.
2. Download the latest **The Unrelenting** release archive from this repository, or use a provided development ZIP.
3. Extract it. Place the **`TheUnrelenting`** folder into the **`Mods` directory beside the WorldBox executable** (this folder is created by NeoModLoader). Do **not** confuse it with `worldbox_Data/StreamingAssets/mods`, which is where the NeoModLoader DLL itself is installed.
4. Ensure the directory contains `mod.json`, `Main.cs`, `default_config.json`, `Locales/`, `GameResources/`, and the media folders.
5. Start WorldBox and open the NeoModLoader menu to confirm **The Unrelenting** has loaded.

Expected layout:

```text
worldbox/
├── Mods/
│   └── TheUnrelenting/
│       ├── mod.json
│       ├── Main.cs
│       ├── default_config.json
│       ├── Locales/
│       ├── GameResources/
│       ├── Assets/
│       ├── Audio/
│       └── icon.png
└── worldbox_Data/
    └── StreamingAssets/
        └── mods/
            └── NeoModLoader.dll
```

**Updating:** close the game and **replace the entire `TheUnrelenting` folder** with the newer version. Do not merge files from two releases. Back up important saves before installing experimental builds.

## Quick start

1. Create a disposable world with a few humanoids and ordinary animals.
2. Leave the preset on **Manual**.
3. Give the **Haunted** trait to one character using the game's trait tools.
4. Observe the population. One of the existing creatures may begin behaving strangely and approaching the cursed character.
5. Try the **Normal** preset if you want rare curses to emerge without manual intervention.
6. If you use Worldfall, assume direct control of a Haunted character to experience the pursuit more personally. Immersion effects are optional and may require additional configuration.

A victim killed by the entity dies in-game. **Always test new builds in worlds you can afford to lose.**

## Troubleshooting

**The curse never starts.** Check that you have either manually applied **Haunted** or enabled a preset with automatic Origins. Random Origins only considers eligible living humanoids and does not guarantee an event on every attempt. Use the optional quick-test setting in a disposable world to validate the system.

**The entity appears stuck.** WorldBox navigation and terrain restrictions still apply. In Manual, Realistic, and Normal presets, isolation can intentionally block the hunter. Its host may also perform an occasional vanilla AI action before the mod redirects it.

**Whispers or the 3D death image do not appear.** Check `Immersion` settings and verbose logs. Worldfall-specific cues require the mod to positively identify the controlled actor. Music and death-screen integration are experimental. Audio and image previews can help isolate the problem.

**A character was not cursed after a witnessed death.** The Witness and Trauma Witness use conservative evidence and eligible-target rules. Not every death is classified as violent, and proximity alone does not guarantee a transmission.

**A descendant was not cursed.** Bloodline needs a verifiable parent–child relationship, an eligible living heir, and the configured probability to pass. Simply sharing a family, city, or surname is not sufficient.

### Linux logs

WorldBox's `Player.log` is commonly available at:

```text
~/.config/unity3d/mkarpenko/WorldBox/Player.log
```

To inspect the mod's events:

```bash
grep -nE 'THE UNRELENTING:|error CS[0-9]+|Failed to compile' \
  ~/.config/unity3d/mkarpenko/WorldBox/Player.log | tail -n 120
```

When reporting a bug, include the **game build, mod version, NeoModLoader version, optional Worldfall version, steps to reproduce, and the relevant log lines**. Avoid publishing unrelated personal information from the full log.
